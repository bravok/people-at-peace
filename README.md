You may be unable to do this for some time. 
The weather may not permit it. 
Your location may have no nighttime sky.

where the sky cannot be seen.

# About Peace

Everything you need to know to have Peace may be found inside of you and 
in Your relationships with Your Loved-Ones and Friends.

If You have no Loved Ones You have no Peace, but have depression.

If You have no Friends You have no Peace, but have anxiety.

This is the way of Homo Sapiens Sapien.

# The Way

The way to Peace is illuminated with four Guidance.

## The First Guidance

The First Guidance is as follows:

There are four categories of people in the world among and including you.

The first group is your Self; past, present and future. Every version of You that ever was, and every version of You that is yet to come. It may seem that You your Self are only one person, but this is not the case. You are an infinite number of people, each marking the passage of time.

The second group are your Loved-Ones. These are the 10-12 people that are closest to you; your spouse, your child, your parent, their loved-ones. There are no more than 12. This is the way of Home Sapiens Sapien.

The third group are Your friends. These are the 29 to 31 people that you spend your time with; and their friends or loved ones. There are no more than 31 required to bring peace to your life. This is the way of Home Sapiens Sapien.

The fourth group are all of the Others. Your coworkers. The man in the car behind you. The woman and child sitting on the bus seat in front of you. Your Teacher. The Strangers soliciting you for your time, talent, or treasure.

## The Second Guidance

Treat each of these four differently.

### Your Self

Control your Self. Do not give up control to Others. 

Advocate for your Self. 

### Your Loved-Ones

Love your Loved-Ones unconditionally. You chose them. Some times they are family; other times they are not.

Be forgiving, be understanding to these people. We each need a small group of people who will always be kind to us, no matter what. Your Loved-Ones are those people. Give them the same in return.

### Your Friends

Support your Friends. They are not as close to you as your Loved Ones, but should also receive your forgiveness.

Give feedback to your Friends. Tell them when you think they are making a mistake. This is the very relationship that permits you to do so. Expect the same from them.

### All Others

For all Others, treat them respectfully, but appropriately.

You owe Others nothing. You work with them only to the benefit of your Self, your Friends, and your Loved Ones.

There is simply no reason to regard their opinions about you as if they were from the people closer to you. And there is no reason for You to assert your opinions on Others.

Expect them to mind their own business, and mind your own.

To be rude about it is to invite conflict or interactions. These take away from your time and energy for your Self, your Friends, and your Loved Ones. Be as respectful as required to make any interactions as brief and beneficial as possible.

Others have their own Selves, Friends and Loved-Ones. Expect and insist that they focus on their own control; their own relationships.

## The Third Guidance

The number of people you regard as Friends and Loved Ones matters!

## The Fourth Guidance

When your group grows too large, divide it into two equal halves. This is the way of life. The healthy cell divides into two equal halves. The cancer fails to divide or does not divide equally. Cancer is deadly over time.
